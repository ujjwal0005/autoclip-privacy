AutoClip Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

What AutoClip accesses

AutoClip asks for one Google permission: youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the YouTube channel of the person who signs in. It does not read, change or delete any other YouTube or Google account data.

How data is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. AutoClip has no server and does not send Google user data to anyone other than Google's own YouTube API.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

AutoClip's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke AutoClip's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the local token file.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/ujjwal0005/autoclip-privacy.
